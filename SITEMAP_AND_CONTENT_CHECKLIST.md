### 📝 Content Guidelines

#### Writing Style
- **Tone**: Professional, calm, patient-friendly
- **Language**: Non-technical, accessible to all reading levels
- **Length**: Keep content concise and scannable
- **Format**: Use short paragraphs, bullet points where appropriate

#### Medical Content
- **No Medical Advice**: Do not provide diagnosis or treatment advice
- **Disclaimers**: Include appropriate disclaimers where needed
- **Accuracy**: Ensure all medical information is accurate and approved

#### Contact Information
- **Consistency**: Use same format for phone/fax across all pages
- **Accessibility**: Ensure phone numbers are clickable (tel: links)
- **Verification**: Double-check all contact information for accuracy

## Content Submission Format

When providing content, you can use this format:

### Example: Provider Information
```
Provider 1:
- Name: Dr. John Smith
- Title: MD
- Bio: Dr. John Smith is a board-certified physician specializing in pain intervention. He received his medical degree from [University] and has been practicing for [X] years. He is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-centered care.
- Photo: [Attach photo file or provide path]
```

### Example: FAQ
```
Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: To book an appointment, please call our office at [phone number] during business hours (9:00 AM – 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday). Our staff will be happy to assist you in scheduling a convenient time.
```

### Example: Form Link
```
Form Name: New Patient Intake Form
Link: https://example.com/forms/intake.pdf
Description: Please complete this form before your first visit.
```

## Notes

- **Placeholders**: The site will be built with clear placeholders (e.g., `[Phone Number]`) so you can easily identify what needs to be filled in
- **Incremental Updates**: Content can be added incrementally - the site will work with placeholders, but should be completed before launch
- **File Formats**: 
  - Images: JPG, PNG, or SVG
  - PDFs: PDF format
  - Logos: SVG (preferred) or PNG with transparent background
- **File Sizes**: 
  - Images should be optimized (under 500KB recommended)
  - PDFs should be reasonable size (under 5MB recommended)
