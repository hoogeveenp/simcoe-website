The Simcoe Clinic

Specializing in Pain Intervention

Our mission is to help people with chronic pain live better. We provide a comprehensive patient assessment and multidisciplinary approach to treatment. Recommendations may include lifestyle changes, physical rehabilitation, psychological therapy, and medication use. Interventional techniques are utilized where appropriate. We are available for consultation through referral from your family doctor. Referral forms can be downloaded from our forms page.
